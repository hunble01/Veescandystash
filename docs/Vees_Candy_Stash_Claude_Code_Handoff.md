# Vee’s Candy Stash — Claude Code Business + Website Brief

*Every Sweet Tooth Needs a Stash.*

The complete business + website vision · Detailed reference document and Claude Code build brief · Prepared October 21, 2026

**Purpose.** Give Claude Code the business context it does not have: what this candy shop is, what customers should be able to buy, how regular and themed candy bags differ, how custom orders should work, what the brand should feel like, and what to build into a polished, modern website.

**Important:** This document records decisions from our discussions and organizes additional website recommendations. It is not a claim that every product, size, price, policy, or technical feature is finalized. Where necessary, items are marked confirmed, planned, or to decide.

## 1. The business in one page

- **Final working brand:** Vee’s Candy Stash — the personal, memorable version using “Vee,” connected to Vanessa. Earlier drafts used “The Candy Stash” and “V’s Candy Stash”; the website should use Vee’s Candy Stash consistently.
- **Confirmed slogan:** “Every Sweet Tooth Needs a Stash.”
- **Core concept:** A small, Ontario-based candy business selling attractive, fun, easy-to-order candy bags, including everyday pick-and-mix selections and themed or personalized candy favours for events.
- **Start small, grow intentionally:** Begin with a curated number of candy choices, simple shelves and packing, and a manageable menu; expand products, event designs, and capacity as demand grows.
- **Main customer groups:** People buying treats for themselves, gifts, parents hosting children’s birthdays, baby showers and gender reveals, weddings and bridal showers, holiday celebrations, and hosts ordering favours in bulk.
- **Shop personality:** Fun, cute, upbeat, thoughtful, welcoming, modern, slightly retro and photo-worthy, but easy to shop and not overwhelming.
- **Business model:** Direct-to-customer online orders, plus personalized orders for celebrations; local fulfilment options and service area need to be confirmed before enabling checkout.

## 2. Brand identity and art direction

### Logo and visual direction — decisions to preserve

- Use the user’s actual chosen logo asset when it is supplied. Do not invent a different logo or replace it with a generic candy illustration.
- The preferred logo direction is playful and retro: “Vee’s” smaller above the larger “Candy Stash” wordmark; orange and pink lettering, teal/turquoise accents, and possibly a smiley/candy detail. Exact final design should come from the supplied logo file.
- **Palette:** warm orange, pink and a touch of teal with white/cream neutrals. This is a brand palette, not a request for an all-pink interface.
- Avoid rainbow-themed branding, overly childish clutter, and pink covering every section. White space, clean product photography, rounded shapes and small playful flourishes are preferred.
- The website, Instagram visuals, bags, pre-printed stickers, thank-you cards and event materials should all feel coordinated.

### Copywriting voice

- Warm, short, playful and confident. Clear shopping instructions are more important than excessive puns or long blocks of text.
- **Example copy:** “Your sweet little stash starts here.” / “Pick your favourites.” / “Made sweeter for your celebration.” / “A stash for every occasion.”

Brand references should be treated as style guidance, not substitutes for the final logo. The business has explored white/cream backgrounds, soft orange/pink/teal accents and lively candy imagery.

## 3. Product strategy: everyday candy bags

**Flagship offering:** Candy bags for everyday gifting, snacking, parties or just because. These must be simple to understand, visual, and quick to buy. The bags can feature mixed candy or customer-selected combinations.

### Regular bag options

- **Signature Mixed Stash:** A curated assortment combining a variety of candy types. Good first-time purchase and gift option.
- **Sour Stash:** A mix focused on sour candies such as sour belts/strips and sour gummies.
- **Sweet Stash:** Non-sour gummies and other sweeter favourites.
- **Sweet + Sour Stash:** A combined assortment.
- **Build Your Own Stash:** Choose bag size and available candy selections, with a visible progress indicator or number-of-picks allowance according to the eventual pricing model.
- **Possible later extensions:** Mini favour bags, premium seasonal mixtures, candy boxes and other gift formats. Do not launch too many formats at once.

### Sizes and assortment — not yet finalized

Previously brainstormed: mini, small, medium and large. The actual weight in grams, candy count, allowable number of mixes, packaging dimensions and retail prices should be decided only after sourcing and testing. The website must store these as editable data, not hard-code pretend numbers.

Start with approximately 10–15 candy varieties if manageable, then scale toward a larger candy wall as sales justify it. Sample candy categories discussed: gummy bears, peach rings, sour watermelon, sour peaches, sour belts, gummies, other classic sweet and sour candies, and possible chocolates. These are examples, not a confirmed ingredient or inventory list.

## 4. Themed candy bags for parties and occasions

This is a major part of the business, not a small add-on. Vee’s Candy Stash should offer candy bags made specifically for celebrations. A themed order should look intentional and gift-ready, with colour-coordinated candy choices where available, matching labels/tags, and an optional personalized message.

| Collection | Possible customer-facing offering |
| --- | --- |
| Birthdays + kids’ parties | Individual party favour bags; age/theme colours; birthday name/date and “thank you” stickers; mini guest favours or larger treat bags. |
| Baby showers | Soft/pastel or selected colour themes, “Baby on the Way” style messages, personalized guest favour labels. |
| Gender reveals | Pink/blue or custom-theme candy assortments; party favours with host-approved reveal wording or colour presentation. |
| Weddings + engagements | Elegant coordinated favour bags; initials, names, date; optional ribbons or tags. |
| Bridal showers + bachelorettes | Shower-themed guest favours, custom event tags and coordinated colours. |
| Baptisms + dedications | Tasteful personalized favours for family and faith celebrations when requested. |
| Holidays | Special limited-time collections for Halloween, Christmas, Valentine’s Day, Easter and other seasonal moments. |
| Other celebrations | Graduations, anniversaries, teacher gifts, corporate gatherings, school events and celebrations by request. |

**Important distinction:** “Shop by Occasion” can contain ready-to-order themed collections and a clear path to fully customized orders. Customers must not confuse a standard bag, a themed preset, and a bespoke event order.

## 5. Personalized and custom-order experience

Let customers create something for their exact event. Custom orders should work for a small personal gift or for dozens of matching favours for a celebration, subject to the eventual minimum quantity and lead time.

### What customers may personalize

- Occasion and theme (birthday, wedding, holiday, baby shower, gender reveal, or other).
- Colours or overall visual style, with a note that candy availability can limit exact colour matching.
- Preferred sweet/sour assortment and ingredient/allergen preferences where operationally supportable.
- Quantity of bags or favours; selected portion/bag size; target event date.
- Label or sticker message: names, date, a thank-you message or short custom wording.
- Packaging enhancements: logo or occasion sticker, tag, ribbon, thank-you card or simple gift box, where offered.
- Budget range, desired pickup/delivery area, and extra instructions or reference photos.

### Suggested custom-order journey

1. Customer opens Custom Orders or Shop Events.
2. Chooses the occasion.
3. Enters date, quantity and bag size.
4. Selects candy preference and colour theme.
5. Adds personalized label text and packaging choices.
6. Views a summary.
7. Submits an inquiry or places an order if the pricing is reliable and dates are available.
8. Owner confirms details, final quote and fulfilment instructions.

**Launch-safe recommendation:** For elaborate or bulk custom event orders, begin with a polished request-a-quote form rather than pretending all combinations have fixed instant prices. Later, automated event pricing can be added after true costs, minimums and production capacity are established.

## 6. Packaging and unboxing

Packaging should be recognizable, cute, consistent and affordable to assemble. The owner’s current preference is simple white packaging with her own branding, though clear resealable food-safe candy bags are also being considered for showcasing the mix.

- **Everyday orders:** food-safe clear resealable bags or suitable white bags/pouches, finished with a branded Vee’s Candy Stash sticker.
- **Custom event orders:** colour/themed sticker or tag with host-approved message, possibly a ribbon or other small decorative accent. Keep upgrades optional and price them separately.
- **Order insert:** a small thank-you card, possibly double-sided with business details/social handle on the reverse. Final copy and design are not settled.
- **Optional presentation:** white gift boxes or tissue for larger gifts, premium orders and event bundles; not necessary for every single bag.
- **Sticker decision:** Order pre-printed labels from a professional printer instead of printing them at home. Circle and square shapes were explored; glossy finishes were considered, but no final supplier, size or finish is approved.
- All food-contact materials and seals should be appropriate for food handling; any label needs the legally required information based on the actual products and Ontario/Canadian rules.

### Packaging content the website should show

Clear product photos showing the actual bag style, approximate scale, flavour assortment and any occasion label options. If photography is not ready, use tasteful placeholder frames that are clearly not the finished product and are easy to replace.

## 7. Physical workspace and operating plan

The business is intended to begin in a basement home workspace with a tidy, simple set-up. The exercise equipment would be moved out of the selected area. The preferred layout uses the back white wall, two central shelving units and one table as the main candy display and packing station. Storage equipment belongs separately to the right rather than crowding the central display.

- Simple shelves, potentially basic Walmart-style units; preference for white but not fixed. The owner wants inexpensive pieces and expects to expand gradually.
- Transparent lidded candy containers/jars on the shelves, with room for more flavours later; clean matching scoops/tongs and easy-to-read labels.
- A worktable for weighing, assembling, sealing, labeling and checking orders.
- Digital gram scale, food-safe scoops/tongs, suitable cleaning products and clearly documented cleaning procedures between orders.
- Airtight storage and sensible stock rotation with lot/batch and best-before tracking so the online inventory stays accurate.
- Source candy, containers, packaging, racks and labels at practical Canadian prices; startup purchasing should remain lean.

**Not a finalized operations plan:** Ontario public-health requirements, business registration, food handling, packaging/labelling, online sales and any residential-use rules must be checked before accepting commercial food orders. The website should not claim regulatory approval or “allergen-free” status without verification.

## 8. Website goals and what “world-class” means here

The goal is a polished, credible consumer storefront — playful enough to feel like a candy brand, professional enough that customers confidently buy gifts and event favours. It should look excellent on an iPhone and be effortless to manage as a solo founder.

- **Immediate goals:** Build brand awareness; show a beautiful candy range; convert standard-bag purchases; generate themed and custom-order inquiries; explain fulfilment; connect customers with the owner.
- **Customer experience:** Mobile-first, fast loading, engaging candy imagery, large tappable buttons, logical filters, clear ingredient/allergen disclosures, and few distractions.
- **Business experience:** Ability to update product availability, pricing, options, holiday collections and photos without rewriting the website.
- **Trust:** Authentic business details, accurate policies, clear turnaround expectations, secure checkout when enabled, simple contact options and FAQ.
- **Distinctiveness:** A candy-stash visual motif, a playful mix builder, striking but disciplined colours, and seasonal mini-collections that can be switched on/off.

## 10. Homepage layout: design direction

### Suggested homepage scroll sequence

1. **Header:** Logo left, simple navigation (Shop, Build Your Stash, Occasions, Custom Orders, About), cart icon and mobile menu. Optional announcement strip for new collections, local ordering or seasonal windows — do not advertise unsupported promises.
2. **Hero:** Headline “Every Sweet Tooth Needs a Stash.” Friendly supporting line such as “Sweet little bags, party-ready favours and custom candy made for every occasion.” Primary button “Shop Candy Bags”; secondary button “Create Your Stash”. Show a bright, authentic candy/packaging photograph.
3. **Explore:** Four visual entry cards: Mixed Stash, Sour Stash, Sweet Stash, Build Your Own.
4. **Shop by Occasion:** Visual collection tiles for Birthday, Baby Shower, Gender Reveal, Wedding and Holidays, with a “View All Occasions” link.
5. **How it works:** Pick a bag or theme; personalize your order; choose fulfilment/submit order.
6. **Custom order callout:** “Planning something sweet?” with a prominent Request a Custom Order button.
7. **Closing:** Founder brand sentence, newsletter signup only if email marketing is properly configured, then a strong footer with policies and contact.

## 11. Shop, product page and candy builder details

### Product card fields

Photo, name, short descriptor, portion/size, price once set, availability, optional sweet/sour label, and a prominent Choose Options button. Never show arbitrary review ratings, fabricated stock or unconfirmed “best seller” status.

### Product detail

Photo gallery, what is included, contents/ingredients/allergens where required, size/weight, selectable options, quantity, transparent final cost, order timing and packaging note. Explain that candy designs may differ slightly by stock if that is in the owner’s policy.

### Build Your Stash interaction

The builder should use a step-by-step visual interface, not a large overwhelming form. Ideally: (1) size, (2) type or candy mix, (3) select available candy varieties subject to a per-size allowance, (4) packaging/occasion choices if enabled, (5) quantity and cart. Show step progress, selected items and running price. Disable impossible combinations and identify sold-out items.

**Data integrity:** Pricing and candy-pick limits must come from editable product configuration. Do not imply unlimited mixes or promises about certain candies until verified.

## 12. Events and holiday collections

Holiday collections should operate as seasonal drops: visually themed landing pages and pre-configured assortments, launch windows, order cutoff dates and event-specific messaging. A holiday section should automatically support “coming soon,” “available now” and “closed” states set by the owner, without promising stock.

- **Halloween:** Trick-or-treat party favours, Halloween event bags and spooky themed labels.
- **Christmas:** Stocking treats, teacher gifts, giftable candy bags and holiday party favours.
- **Valentine’s Day:** Sweet gifting, classroom or party favours, cute recipient messages.
- **Easter:** Spring colours, treat favours and baskets/bag add-ons if launched.
- **Year-round special events:** Weddings, birthdays and baby celebrations must stay discoverable even when seasonal holiday content rotates.

Photos, candy combinations, names, colour themes and availability are examples to plan the site; specific products should only be published when operationally ready.

## 13. Order and fulfilment flows

| Order type | Recommended launch workflow |
| --- | --- |
| Regular ready-to-order bags | Product → choose size/mix → quantity → cart → checkout (when payments/fulfilment are configured) → confirmation. |
| Builder bags | Builder → valid candy selection → quantity → cart → checkout → detailed order record and inventory deduction. |
| Preset party/theme bags | Occasion collection → style or personalisation fields → quantity → customer confirms lead time → checkout or approval step. |
| Full bespoke bulk event orders | Custom request form → owner reviews and quotes → customer approves → deposit/payment only after policy and tools are established → production → pickup/delivery. |

**Key operational rules — all to decide:** Order minimums, deposits, final payment timing, change windows, rush fees, pickup/delivery area, delivery charges, shipping eligibility, minimum lead time for events, cancellation/refund policy, and tax setup. Present as editable settings and clear placeholders, not invented promises.

## 14. Site functionality and admin priorities

### Must-have for a convincing first version

- Responsive mobile-first page layouts and accessible navigation, usable on older and newer phones.
- Consistent sitewide brand components: header, footer, buttons, product cards, product tiles and form fields.
- Product catalogue with editable name, category, description, images, price, candy composition and stock/availability.
- Shop by Occasion section with a content-managed set of collections.
- Detailed custom-order form with validation, accessible error messages and an owner notification workflow.
- Cart experience with clear price totals; connect real payments only when a payment provider and account are available.
- An admin-friendly way to update inventory, price, sizes, photos and seasonal visibility.
- Basic SEO metadata, structured product information where appropriate, image optimisation, analytics and clear privacy/consent notices.

### Strong phase-two enhancements

- Visual mix builder with candy images and custom rules, saved carts or favourites when justified.
- Live event-order status, automatic reminder emails and optional digital proofs of label designs.
- Seasonal landing-page scheduling, gift messages, bundles and promo codes.
- Product reviews/testimonials once real customer permission and data exist.

## 16. Content, photography and brand deliverables needed

- **Provide to Claude Code:** The actual Vee’s Candy Stash logo in PNG/SVG if available; do not generate a replacement.
- **Photography:** Product shots of real candy mixtures, bags with the actual logo, clear jars/candy display, themed labels, close-up textures and event favour sets. Good photos matter more than decorative animations.
- **Product data:** Confirmed candy options, bag sizes in grams, prices, stock, allergen/ingredient disclosures and packing details.
- **Business logistics:** Confirmed contact email/social handle, relevant service area, accepted fulfilment methods, order timings and payment processor.

**Until assets exist:** Use clearly labeled editable placeholders, preserve the intended design, and make asset replacement straightforward. Do not invent a logo, customer testimonials, sale prices, company address, legal claims or credentials.

## 18. Recommended build order for Claude Code

**Phase 1 — Design system and clickable storefront:** Establish colours, typography, logo component placeholder, navigation, home page, shop catalogue, themed collection pages, detailed product page, About, FAQ and Contact. Use representative mock content clearly marked as draft.

**Phase 2 — Core commerce and enquiries:** Implement size/option selectors, cart, robust custom event form, a safe lead-management/notification path, and owner-editable product data. Add real payment and fulfilment options only after owner provides accounts, prices and policies.

**Phase 3 — Interactive builder:** Build the controlled Build Your Stash experience with valid candy-option constraints, pricing, inventory connections and excellent mobile UX.

**Phase 4 — Polish and launch preparation:** Replace draft imagery/content, confirm accessibility, test on mobile, performance optimisation, SEO, final policy/food-labelling review, analytics and transaction/order testing.

## 19. Copy-paste instructions for Claude Code

The following is the implementation instruction that should accompany this brief. It deliberately leaves unsettled operational facts configurable:

**PROJECT:** Build a premium, mobile-first, visually distinctive e-commerce website for Vee’s Candy Stash, an Ontario-based, founder-led candy bag and event favour business. Use the tagline “Every Sweet Tooth Needs a Stash.” The business sells everyday curated and customizable candy bags, occasion-themed party favours, holiday collections, and bespoke event orders.

**BRANDING:** Playful retro-candy energy, polished rather than childish. Light white/cream backgrounds, warm orange and pink with restrained turquoise accents, rounded UI and beautiful candy photography. Avoid rainbow branding, pink-heavy clutter and generic templates. Integrate the actual owner-provided logo; do not substitute or recreate it.

**SHOPPING:** Support signature mixed/sour/sweet bags, a future Build Your Stash stepper, categories, occasion pages, product variants, cart and eventually checkout. Customers should be able to easily find birthday, baby shower, gender reveal, wedding, bridal shower and holiday candy favours.

**CUSTOMIZATION:** Build an excellent custom-event inquiry journey with quantity, date, theme/colours, preferred candy mix, size, packaging, label wording, budget and contact details. Start complex bespoke requests with quotes rather than fabricated prices.

**ENGINEERING:** Responsive, accessible, high-performance, polished animation only where it helps. Build reusable components and data-driven product/collection models. Provide straightforward content editing, sensible validation, error/loading/empty states, image optimization and SEO. Do not invent actual stock, delivery promises, prices, reviews, regulated food claims or legal policies. Keep those in clearly indicated configuration/data fields. Use practical modern technology appropriate for a maintainable small business, and clearly list any required credentials and hosting/payment decisions.

**WORKFLOW:** First propose sitemap, component architecture, design tokens, product schema and implementation phases from this brief. Then implement the best functional MVP. Demonstrate how the owner updates products and themes, and document remaining decisions before live orders can launch.

## 20. Final north star

The website should feel like opening a sweet little stash. An ordinary visitor should immediately understand that Vee’s Candy Stash offers delightful candy bags. An event host should immediately see that birthdays, baby showers, weddings, gender reveals, holidays and custom designs are a core specialty. Both shoppers should be able to act within a few taps. Above all, the experience should be unmistakably Vee’s Candy Stash — memorable, clean, fun and easy to trust.
